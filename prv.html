Your Privacy Matters

Privacy Policy

How Taste Siem Reap collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you visit our website or use our contact form.

📅 Last updated: January 2026

At Taste Siem Reap, we believe your privacy should be respected. This policy explains, in plain language, what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we keep it safe.

By using our website, you agree to the practices described here. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us — we're happy to help.

01.Information We Collect

We only collect the information we actually need to serve you well. This includes:

Information you give us

  • Contact form: your name, email address, subject, and message when you send us a message.
  • Restaurant suggestions: any details you share when recommending a place.

Information we collect automatically

  • Basic analytics: anonymized data about how visitors use our site (pages viewed, time on page, device type). We use this to improve the site.
  • Language preference: your chosen language is stored locally in your browser (localStorage) so we can show the site in your language next time.

We do not collect sensitive personal information, and we do not sell your data to anyone.

02.How We Use Your Information

Your information is used only for the following purposes:

  • To reply to you: we use your email address to respond to messages you send us.
  • To improve the site: anonymized analytics help us understand which pages are useful and which need work.
  • To remember your preferences: so the site loads in your chosen language.
  • To keep the site safe: we may use basic tools to prevent spam or abuse of the contact form.

We will never use your data for purposes unrelated to these. We do not run advertising, and we do not send marketing emails unless you ask us to.

03.Cookies & Tracking

Taste Siem Reap uses a very light approach to cookies and local storage:

  • Local storage (no cookie): your language preference (English, Khmer, or Chinese) is saved in your browser's localStorage — not a cookie, and never sent to our servers.
  • Analytics: if we use an analytics tool, it may set cookies to count unique visitors anonymously. You can block cookies in your browser settings if you prefer.

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party retargeting. You can clear your local storage at any time through your browser settings.

04.Sharing Your Information

We keep your information private. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to anyone. Period.

The only situations in which we might share information are:

  • Service providers: trusted tools we use to run the site (for example, a contact form service like Formspree or EmailJS). These providers only see what's necessary to deliver the service.
  • Legal requirements: if required by law, we may need to disclose information to authorities. We will only do so when legally obligated.

05.Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information:

  • Encrypted connections: our website uses HTTPS, so data sent through the contact form is encrypted in transit.
  • Limited collection: we don't collect more than we need, which reduces risk.
  • Trusted providers: any third-party service we use is chosen for its strong security and privacy practices.

However, no website is 100% secure. If you have sensitive information to share, please be cautious when sending it online.

06.Your Rights

You have full control over your personal information. You can ask us to:

  • Access: tell you what information we hold about you.
  • Correct: fix any inaccurate information.
  • Delete: remove your information from our systems.
  • Withdraw consent: ask us to stop using your information.

To exercise any of these rights, just contact us. We'll respond within a reasonable time and always aim to make it easy for you.

07.Children's Privacy

Our website is designed for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has shared information with us, please contact us and we'll remove it promptly.

08.Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. When we do, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this page occasionally. Continued use of our website after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Questions About Your Privacy?

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests about how we handle your data, please reach out — we're happy to help.
